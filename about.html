Nestled amid lush greenery on Harmony Farm Land at Manaveli, near Auroville, Re Leaf Resort is a peaceful nature resort in Pondicherry. Stay in our spacious three-bedroom AC and Non-AC cottages, cool off in the swimming pool and let the kids enjoy the play area — a peaceful resort in Pondicherry where families enjoy a relaxing resort stay, couples find a couple-friendly resort in Pondicherry that doubles as a romantic getaway resort, and teams use it as a corporate retreat resort.
