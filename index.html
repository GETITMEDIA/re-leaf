Your Peaceful Escape
Into Nature

Re Leaf Resort Pondicherry is a nature-inspired family resort set on Harmony Farm Land at Manaveli, near Auroville. With spacious AC and Non-AC cottages, a swimming pool and a children’s play area, it offers a comfortable and relaxing stay for families and groups.

Planning a weekend getaway in Pondicherry? Re Leaf Resort offers a peaceful stay surrounded by greenery, with comfortable cottages, a swimming pool, and a children’s play area. Its location near Auroville and the coast makes it a convenient choice for families and groups looking to relax and spend quality time together.

  • Spacious Cottages for Up to 12 Guests.
  • Swimming Pool & Children's Play Area.
  • Private Balconies with Scenic Views.
