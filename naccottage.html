About Non-AC Cottage

Enjoy a comfortable group stay in our spacious naturally ventilated cottage, thoughtfully designed across three bedrooms to host up to 12 guests — perfect for large families, groups of friends, and leisure travelers. Cross-ventilation and ceiling fans keep every room fresh, while the cottage maintains a peaceful, natural atmosphere throughout your stay — Re Leaf is an eco-friendly resort in Pondicherry and a family vacation resort at heart, making this cottage ideal for a longer stay at a vacation resort in Pondicherry.
